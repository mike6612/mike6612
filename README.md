### Hi, I'm Michael ! 


My passion for creating software solutions began when I had the privilege of attending Harvard Summer School through Summer of a Lifetime, where I was a part of a pre-college Harvard curriculum focused on computer science. It was an ineffable experience. Interacting with classmates sharing similar interests sparked my ambition and enthusiasm for developing code. This soldified my desire to pursue a career in software development. 

This curiosity followed me through my pursuit of higher education at the University of Illinois Chicago, where I became comfortable working with several programming languages and continue to build a strong foundation in software principles and technical skills. Currently, I am working with React and Three.js to develop an innovative platform, [Exoplanets](https://github.com/mike6612/Exoplanets), that educates individuals about the exoplanetary systems of our galaxy :milky_way:. This project allows me to combine my passion for coding with my interest in space exploration.
